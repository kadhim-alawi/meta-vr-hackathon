Tips
- Lighting: bright, even room light improves both hand tracking and passthrough quality.
- Cut the folding shots tight. Show 2–3 folds in full, then use a time-lapse dissolve for the rest.
- Keep music from the app (the generative music box) as the soundtrack; it's original.
- Include a 2–3 second clip of the *Skip this fold* menu to show accessibility.
